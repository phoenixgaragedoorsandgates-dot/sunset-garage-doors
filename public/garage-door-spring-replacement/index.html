Garage Door Spring Replacement in Monrovia, CA

Call (626) 606-9766 Text Us

A broken spring is the most common garage door repair we do. Springs do the heavy lifting; your opener just guides the door. When one snaps, the door suddenly becomes too heavy to open.

We replace torsion and extension springs, match them to your door's weight, and balance the door so it moves smoothly and your opener isn't strained. Covered by our 1-year repair warranty.

ISigns you need this

When to call us

  • You heard a loud bang from the garage
  • There's a visible gap in the spring above the door
  • The door opens a few inches, then stops
  • The door feels extremely heavy by hand
  • Cables are loose or hanging

On two-spring doors, we usually recommend replacing both springs at once. They wear at the same rate, so when one breaks, the other is usually close behind. We'll tell you the options and you decide.

Spring Replacement FAQ

Can I open my garage door with a broken spring?

We don't recommend it. Without the spring, the door's full weight is on you or the opener. Keep the door closed and call us.

Torsion vs. extension springs: what's the difference?

Torsion springs mount on a bar above the door and twist to lift it. Extension springs run along the horizontal tracks and stretch. Both store a lot of tension and should be replaced by a technician.
