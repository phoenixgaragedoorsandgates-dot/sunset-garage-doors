Garage Door Off Track? What to Do (and What Not to Do)

Our off-track repair service

A garage door that has slipped off its track is more than an inconvenience. The door is heavy, under spring tension, and no longer properly supported. Here's what causes it and what to do.

Common causes

  • Impact. Backing into the door or hitting the track bends it out of line.
  • Broken cable. When a lift cable snaps, one side of the door drops and the rollers can pop out.
  • Worn rollers. Old, wobbly rollers can ride out of the track.
  • Loose track brackets. Tracks that pull away from the wall widen the gap the rollers ride in.
  • Obstructions. Something in the track can force a roller out.

What to do right away

  1. Stop using the door and unplug the opener so nobody presses the button.
  2. Keep people, pets and cars away from the door.
  3. Don't try to force the door up or down or hammer the track back in place.
  4. Take a photo of both sides and call or text a technician.

How we fix it

We secure the door, release tension safely where needed, put the rollers back in the track, straighten or replace bent track sections, and replace broken cables or worn rollers. Then we test the balance and the opener's safety reverse so the door runs right.
