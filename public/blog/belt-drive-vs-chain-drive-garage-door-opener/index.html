Belt Drive vs. Chain Drive Garage Door Openers

Our opener replacement service

When it's time for a new garage door opener, the first choice is usually belt drive or chain drive. Both lift the door the same way. The differences are mostly about noise and cost.

Chain drive

A metal chain pulls a trolley that moves the door. Chain drives are durable and usually the lower-cost option. They're louder, which is fine for a detached garage or one that's away from bedrooms.

Belt drive

A reinforced rubber belt replaces the chain. Belt drives are much quieter and smoother, which makes them the popular choice when there's a bedroom or living space above or next to the garage.

Other features worth considering

  • Smart control: open, close and check your door from your phone.
  • Battery backup: keeps the door working during a power outage.
  • Wall-mount (jackshaft) openers: mount beside the door and free up ceiling space.
  • Keypads and extra remotes for family members.

Which should you choose?

If the garage is attached and you care about noise, choose a belt drive. If it's detached or noise doesn't matter, a chain drive does the job for less. We install LiftMaster and Chamberlain openers and can help you pick the right model.
