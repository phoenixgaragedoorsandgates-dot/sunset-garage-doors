We don't recommend it. Garage door springs hold enough tension to lift a door that can weigh a few hundred pounds, and releasing that tension without the right tools can cause serious injury. Leave the door closed and call a technician.

Garage door repair questions

Why is my garage door making so much noise?

Common causes are worn rollers, loose hardware, dry moving parts or springs near the end of their life. A tune-up and new nylon rollers often make a door much quieter.

My door opens a few inches and stops. What's wrong?

That's often a broken spring or a misadjusted opener force setting. Don't keep running the opener; it can damage the opener and the door. Call or text us a photo.

Can I open my garage door with a broken spring?

We don't recommend it. Without the spring, the door's full weight is on you or the opener. Keep the door closed and call us.

Torsion vs. extension springs: what's the difference?

Torsion springs mount on a bar above the door and twist to lift it. Extension springs run along the horizontal tracks and stretch. Both store a lot of tension and should be replaced by a technician.

Why does my garage door go back up when I try to close it?

Most often the safety sensors near the floor are misaligned, dirty or blocked. It can also be the force settings. If cleaning and lining up the sensors doesn't fix it, call us.

Can I push my door back on the track myself?

It's not safe. The door is under spring tension and can shift or fall. Leave it where it is and call us.

Can I get financing on a new garage door?

Yes, through Acorn Finance. Call us and we'll walk you through it.

Can you replace my old one-piece door with a sectional door?

In many garages, yes. We'll look at your opening and tell you what fits.

Why did my garage door cable come off the drum?

Common causes are a broken spring, a door that hit something while closing, worn cables, or the door being pulled down unevenly. We re-seat or replace the cable and fix whatever caused it.

Should both cables be replaced at once?

Usually, yes. Both cables wear at the same rate, so if one is frayed or broken, the other is often close behind.

Belt drive or chain drive?

Belt drives are much quieter, which matters if there's a bedroom above or next to the garage. Chain drives are durable and usually cost less. We'll help you choose.

Will new rollers make my door quieter?

Usually, yes. Swapping old steel rollers for nylon ones is one of the easiest ways to quiet a noisy door.

What's the difference between 20, 22 and 24 gauge steel?

Gauge measures steel thickness, and a lower number means thicker steel. 20 gauge is the strongest and most dent-resistant, 22 gauge is a solid middle option, and 24 gauge is lighter and costs less. We'll recommend one based on how the door is used.

Do you install commercial doors for small businesses?

Yes. We install doors for shops, garages, warehouses, storage spaces and other businesses throughout the San Gabriel Valley.

Can you build a one-piece door to fit my opening?

Yes. We custom-make one-piece doors on site, building the frame to the exact size of your garage opening, then install it with new arms and springs.

Are one-piece doors still worth repairing?

Often, yes. Springs and hardware can usually be replaced. If the door itself is rotted or warped, replacement usually makes more sense, and we'll give you both options.

What is a garage door strut?

A strut is a steel brace that bolts horizontally across the inside of a garage door section. It reinforces the panel so it can't bend or sag, and it's used to strengthen doors that are already bent.

Why is the top of my garage door bending?

The opener pulls on the top section every time the door moves. Without a strut, that section can bend over time. A reinforcement strut fixes it.

Can a bent garage door panel be fixed with a strut?

Often, yes. If the panel is bent but not torn apart, a strut can straighten and reinforce it. If the panel is badly cracked or split, it may need to be replaced, and we'll tell you which makes sense.

Should I reprogram my opener after moving into a new home?

Yes. The previous owners may still have remotes. We can clear the old codes and program new remotes for you.
